About me

I’m Samu, a Finnish software developer and ICT student who likes solving practical problems with thoughtful, well-built software. I work across C++, Python and JavaScript, and enjoy taking a project from its first rough idea through to a working result.

At Niemikotisäätiö, I developed a Unity-based workplace simulation and taught 3D modelling. The project called for more than building a virtual space: it meant understanding how people would use it and making the experience clear and useful.

During my Finnish army service, I worked as virtual support at the Hamina Army Academy. I helped develop a virtual battlefield for Arma and taught other conscripts how to work effectively in that environment.

I began my Information and Communication Technologies degree at Metropolia University of Applied Sciences in 2022, building on earlier studies in game development and graphics. My projects have strengthened my skills in algorithms, networking, application development and user interfaces, while giving me plenty of opportunities to learn by making.

Away from the keyboard, I’m usually swimming, skiing, fishing or planning another trip. A skiing holiday in Japan and a summer in Singapore have stayed with me; closer to home, board-game nights with friends regularly turn into competitive rounds of Yu-Gi-Oh or Riichi Mahjong.

I’m independent, self-motivated and easy to work with. I take ownership of a project, ask good questions and keep people laughing along the way. The best part of meeting someone new is still the thing I learn that I didn’t know before.
